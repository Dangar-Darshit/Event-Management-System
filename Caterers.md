#Caterers Management in Event Management

##Role

The main role of the caterer is to provide food and beverage services for events and manage catering requests and bookings through the system.


##Features

- Caterer Registration and Login
- Manage Caterer Profile
- Add and Manage Food Menus
- Set Food Package Prices
- View Catering Requests
- Accept or Reject Requests
- Manage Catering Bookings
- Update Booking Status
- View Event and Customer Details
- Check Payment Status

##Booking Status

"Pending → Confirmed → Preparing → Completed"
